Kickflip

← All Tricks

The kickflip is the first trick most skaters set their sights on after the ollie, and for good reason. It's the ollie with a flick off the heel-side edge of the front foot that rotates the board 360 degrees on its long axis. Once you can kickflip consistently, you've unlocked a huge category of tricks, and it becomes the foundation for varials, kickflip variations, and flip tricks into grinds.

Prerequisites

Extremely consistent ollie Extreme board control Extremely comfortable flick

Common Mistakes

  • Kicking down instead of out, which sends the board into the ground.
  • Flicking too early before the pop is complete.
  • Keeping your back foot on the board, it has to get out of the way.
  • Not jumping high enough, the board needs room to flip.

How To Do It

  1. Set up like an ollie, but with your front foot angled slightly toward the heel edge and closer to the front bolts.
  2. Pop the tail, wait a moment, then flick your front foot outward off the heel-side edge of the nose.
  3. Pull your back foot up and away so the board has room to rotate.
  4. Watch the board spin and catch it with both feet as it comes around.
  5. Land with your feet over the bolts and roll away.
