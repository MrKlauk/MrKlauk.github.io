Irmo Skate Park

← All Spots
★★★★★ 4 / 5 local rating

Irmo Skate Park sits inside Rawls Creek Park and is a great middle ground between a street plaza and a bowl. The flow bowl in the back is mellow enough to learn on but deep enough to keep advanced riders interested. The street section out front has a mix of quarter pipes, a flat rail, and a small set of stairs. Nike SB has hosted events here, so the concrete is well maintained and the locals are welcoming.

Best For

Transition Flow bowl Learning street Weekend sessions

Location

📍 1113 Friarsgate Blvd, Irmo, SC 29063

Local Tips

  1. Mornings are the best time to have the bowl to yourself.
  2. The flat rail gets slick after rain, give it a day to dry.
  3. Parking is free and right next to the park.
  4. Bring water, there's no shade over the street section.
