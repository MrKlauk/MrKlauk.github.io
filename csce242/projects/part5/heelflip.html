Heelflip

← All Tricks

The heelflip is the kickflip's mirror image. Instead of flicking off the heel-side edge with your toe, you flick off the toe-side edge with your heel. Some skaters find heelflips easier than kickflips, others find them much harder. Either way, they're an essential flip trick and often feel more natural on certain trick combinations like heelflip front boards or heelflip noseslides.

Prerequisites

Consistent ollie Some flip trick experience Comfortable riding

Common Mistakes

  • Flicking straight down instead of out off the toe-side edge.
  • Front foot placed too far toward the toe edge before popping.
  • Popping too late and the front foot flicks into the ground.
  • Ensure your back foot is on the balls of your feet, near the heelside of the tail.

How To Do It

  1. Set up like an ollie with your front foot's toe hanging slightly off the toe-side edge.
  2. Pop the tail and flick your front foot outward, using your heel to catch the edge.
  3. Keep your back foot up and away from the board as it flips.
  4. Catch the board with both feet when it completes the rotation.
  5. Absorb the landing with your knees and roll away.
