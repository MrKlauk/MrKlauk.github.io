Nose Grind

← All Tricks

The nose grind is the 50-50 grind's more technical cousin. Only the front truck is on the obstacle, and the tail is kicked up in the air behind you. This means all of your balance is controlled by your front foot and your hips. Nose grinds demand precise weight distribution and a lot of commitment. They look great and are the gateway to nose slides, crooks, and nose manuals.

Prerequisites

Consistent 50-50 grind Strong front truck balance Comfortable popping onto ledges

Common Mistakes

  • Shifting your weight too far forward and falling over the ledge.
  • Not keeping your back foot ready to catch the tail on the way out.
  • Approaching too straight on, which makes locking in harder.
  • Looking down at your feet instead of ahead at the end of the ledge.

How To Do It

  1. Approach the ledge at a slight angle with your weight centered.
  2. Pop an ollie and aim your front truck only onto the ledge.
  3. Keep your back foot hovering over the tail and your front knee bent.
  4. Hold your weight directly over the front truck to stay balanced.
  5. When you're ready, shift your weight back and pop off the ledge.
