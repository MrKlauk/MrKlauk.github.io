Gambrell

← All Spots
★★★★★ 4 / 5 local rating

Gambrell is a big open plaza with a huge variety of stair sets and a large flatground area, which makes it one of the best spots in the city for skaters of any level. Beginners can roll around the flatground and work on basics, while more advanced skaters have plenty of stair sets and ledges to session. It's a big, spread-out spot so it rarely feels crowded.

Best For

Flatground Stair sets All skill levels Warm-ups

Location

📍 5765 Bush River Rd, Columbia, SC

Local Tips

  1. Great warm-up spot before heading to Owens Field.
  2. The biggest stair set is on the far side, scope it first.
  3. Plenty of parking.
  4. Lights stay on late, night sessions are solid.
