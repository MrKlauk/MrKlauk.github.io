Tricks

Learn how to perform various skateboarding tricks.

Ollie

Beginner
Flatground

The foundation of all skateboarding. Pop the tail, slide your front foot up, and level the board in the air.

Kickflip

Intermediate
Flatground

Ollie with a flick off the heel-side toe to spin the board 360° on its long axis. The first real milestone.

Heelflip

Intermediate
Flatground

Like a kickflip but the board flips toe-side. Flick off the heel edge with your front foot going outward.

50-50 Grind

Beginner
Rail/Ledge

Lock both trucks onto a ledge or rail and slide. The entry-level grind — learn this before any other.

Nose Grind

Advanced
Rail/Ledge

Balance the front truck only on the obstacle with the tail kicked up. Requires precise weight distribution.

Tre Flip

Advanced
Flatground

Pop a kickflip while scooping the tail to spin the board 360° backside. Also called a 360 flip.
