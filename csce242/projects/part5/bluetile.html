Bluetile Skate Shop

← All Shops

Bluetile has been in Columbia for over 20 years, with a new location in Charleston established last year. Bluetile is ran by a team of very passionate, down to Earth people who go the extra mile to help people skate. They often give away free stickers, decks, and sometimes completes. They also run events and host competitions at local parks. Bluetile is the best skate shop in Columbia.
