Rail/Ledge Beginner

50-50 Grind

← All Tricks

The 50-50 grind is the entry-level grind and the first trick most skaters learn on a ledge or rail. Both trucks are locked onto the obstacle, and you slide with your weight balanced evenly. It teaches you how to approach a ledge, ollie onto it, balance, and pop off cleanly. Once you have 50-50s on lock, every other grind and slide becomes much easier to learn.

Prerequisites

Consistent rolling ollie Comfortable approaching a ledge Good balance at speed

Common Mistakes

  • Approaching too parallel to the ledge, come in at a slight angle.
  • Leaning too far forward or backward on the ledge and slipping out.
  • Not committing to the ollie and catching the ledge with your wheels.
  • Forgetting to pop off at the end and just rolling off the edge.

How To Do It

  1. Approach the ledge or rail at a slight angle, roughly 30 degrees.
  2. Pop a small ollie and aim to land both trucks on top of the obstacle.
  3. Keep your weight centered and your knees slightly bent to balance.
  4. Slide until you're ready to come off.
  5. Pop a small ollie off the end or just roll off the side.
