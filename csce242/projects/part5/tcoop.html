T-Coop Fountain

← All Spots
★★★★★ 3 / 5 local rating

T-Coop Fountain is a classic Columbia street spot built around a massive 8-stair gap with a rough, weathered landing. The run-up is short and the concrete is cracked, which makes landing bolts a real challenge. This is not a beginner spot by any stretch.

Best For

Big gaps Filming Advanced riders

Location

📍 Bull St, Columbia, SC

Local Tips

  1. Bring a broom, the run-up is covered in debris.
  2. Landing is rough, softer wheels or bigger wheels help.
  3. Skate it early morning to avoid traffic on Bull St.
  4. Not a solo spot, always bring a friend.
