Skate Spots

A curated list of the best spots in Columbia, SC.

Owens Field

Park
Advanced

Large concrete park with bowls, ledges, and a street section. Well-maintained and popular with locals.

📍 1351 Jim Hamilton Blvd, Columbia, SC 29205

Irmo Skate Park

Park
Intermediate

Concrete park in Rawls Creek with a flow bowl, quarter pipes, and street features. Hosts Nike SB events.

📍 1113 Friarsgate Blvd, Irmo, SC 29063

Russell House

Street
Intermediate

Long marble banks alongside the university buildings. Great for carving and manual lines.

📍 Greene St, Columbia, SC

Close Hipp

Street
Beginner

Smoothest concrete in the city in the underground parking lot. Lots of manual pads.

📍 Harden St & Devine St, Columbia, SC

T-Coop Fountain

Street
Advanced

Massive 8-stair gap with rough landing. Not for the faint of heart.

📍 Bull St, Columbia, SC

Gambrell

Street
All Levels

Large variety of stair sets, large area of flatground.

📍 5765 Bush River Rd, Columbia, SC
