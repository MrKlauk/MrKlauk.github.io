Russell House

← All Spots
★★★★★ 4 / 5 local rating

Russell House is the student union building at the University of South Carolina, and the long marble banks out front are some of the smoothest ground in the city. The banks are perfect for carving, slappies, and long manual lines. Security is generally cool as long as you're not there during a big campus event or drawing a crowd. Best sessions are early mornings and weekends when campus is quiet.

Best For

Marble banks Manuals Slappies Filming lines

Location

📍 Greene St, Columbia, SC

Local Tips

  1. Weekend mornings are dead, best time to skate.
  2. Don't skate during football game days, way too crowded.
  3. Keep an eye out for campus security, they'll ask you to leave during events.
  4. Combine with a session at Close Hipp since they're a few blocks apart.
