Grindhouse Skate Shop

← All Shops

Grindhouse is a newer shop on Main Street run entirely by local skaters. The vibe is DIY and welcoming, and they carry a rotating stock of used clothes and used boards at prices that are hard to beat. They also stock custom art decks from local artists. If you're looking for something unique or just want to support a small local business, this is the place. Inventory changes constantly, so it's worth stopping in often.
