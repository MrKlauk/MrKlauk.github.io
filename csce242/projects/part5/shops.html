Skate Shops

Specialty skate shops loved by locals.

Bluetile Skate Shop

Decks, hardware

Columbia's original core skate shop. Riders on the team, deep deck selection, and genuine community energy.

📍 2716 Devine St, Columbia, SC 🕐 Mon–Sat 11am–7pm

Salty's Board Shop

Cruisers, pads

Great shop for buying complete, good quality cruiser boards. Also a great shop to buy pads.

📍 3408 Forest Dr, Columbia, SC 🕐 Mon–Fri 10am–8pm, Sat 10am–6pm

Grindhouse Skate Shop

Apparel, art decks

A small business recently established skate shop ran by locals. Has a large supply of used clothes and boards.

📍 1820 Main St, Columbia, SC 🕐 Tue–Sun 12pm–7pm
