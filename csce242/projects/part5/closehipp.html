Street Beginner

Close Hipp

← All Spots
★★★★★ 5 / 5 local rating

Close Hipp is the underground parking garage under the Close and Hipp buildings downtown, and it has the smoothest concrete in the entire city. The manual pads are perfect for learning nose manuals, fakie manuals, and any trick that needs clean ground. It's covered, so it's a great spot on rainy or brutally hot days. Because it's a parking garage, keep an eye out for cars and don't skate during business hours.

Best For

Manuals Flatground Rainy days Beginners

Location

📍 Harden St & Devine St, Columbia, SC

Local Tips

  1. Go after 6pm or on weekends when the garage is mostly empty.
  2. Watch for cars coming around the corners, they can't see you well.
  3. It's good for skating after it rains, but some oil pots and be slick.
  4. Bring water, it stays really hot down there.
