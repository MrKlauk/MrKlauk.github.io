Tre Flip

← All Tricks

The tre flip, short for 360 flip, is one of the most iconic tricks in skateboarding. It combines the flip of a kickflip with a 360-degree backside shove-it, so the board flips and spins at the same time. It's a huge milestone trick and looks amazing when done well. The key is the back foot scoop, which drives the 360 rotation while the front foot handles the flip. Expect to put in serious time on this one.

Prerequisites

Consistent kickflip Consistent backside 360 shove-it Strong pop and jump height

Common Mistakes

  • Scooping the tail without popping, which kills the height.
  • Flicking the front foot too hard and the board over-rotates the flip.
  • Not scooping hard enough with the back foot, so the spin doesn't happen.
  • Jumping straight up instead of slightly forward, the board moves.

How To Do It

  1. Set up with your back foot on the tail and your front foot toward the front bolts, toes angled slightly heel-side.
  2. Ensure that your weight is so far back on the board that if you shifted any further back, you'd enter a manual.
  3. Pop the tail hard and scoop it backside with your back foot to start the 360 rotation.
  4. As the board pops, flick your front foot off the heel-side edge to start the flip.
  5. Keep your knees high and watch the board rotate and flip beneath you.
  6. Catch the board with both feet and absorb the landing.
