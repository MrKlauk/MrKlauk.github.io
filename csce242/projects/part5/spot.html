Owens Field Skate Park

← All Spots
★★★★★ 4 / 5 local rating

Owens Field Skate Park is the best park in the area. Featuring a large flatground section with a downrail, the park flows into a central line requiring good pumping skills to complete. This is an advanced park, with three large bowls in the back of the park. Owens Field Skate Park has a lot to give for beginners, but becomes appreciated more and more as skill level advances.

Best For

Advanced Bowl skating Banks Night sessions

Location

📍 1351 Jim Hamilton Boulevard, Columbia, SC

Local Tips

  1. Best sessions are late at night, there are plenty of people and it's well lit.
  2. Watch your stuff late at night, little kids tend to steal stuff.
  3. The bowls are intense, be confident before dropping in.
  4. After the park gate closes, you can park in the tennis lot and walk over.
