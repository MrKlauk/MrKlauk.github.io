Salty's Board Shop

← All Shops

Salty's Board Shop is the go-to spot in Columbia for anyone looking to get into cruising or longboarding. They carry a huge selection of complete cruiser boards, soft wheels, and protective gear. The staff is patient and happy to walk beginners through their first setup. If you need pads, a helmet, or a board to just roll around town on, Salty's has you covered. They also do special orders for anything they don't have in stock.
